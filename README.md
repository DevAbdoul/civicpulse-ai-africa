# ⚡ CivicPulse AI — Public Energy & Infrastructure Allocation Tracker

> **OSF × Andela Hackathon 2026 Submission** | *Track: Transparency & Accountability*  
> **Author:** AbdurRazaq Abdulhamid Isa  

CivicPulse AI is an open-source, low-bandwidth civic intelligence platform designed to demystify public energy budgets, rural solar mini-grid allocations, and transformer procurement records for African communities. Using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and local text-to-speech, it converts dense multi-page government PDFs into plain-language summaries backed by verifiable source citations.

---

## 🎯 The Core Problem

Across many African regions, millions of dollars are allocated annually for rural electrification, solar streetlights, and power grid maintenance. However, this critical data remains locked inside complex, 100+ page government procurement gazettes and budget PDFs filled with economic jargon. 

Because rural and peri-urban citizens face literacy, technical, and data bandwidth barriers:
* Communities cannot verify if budgeted energy infrastructure was actually delivered.
* Misinformation and distrust surround public procurement decisions.
* Grassroots leaders lack fast, accessible tools to hold local institutions accountable.

---

## 💡 The Solution: CivicPulse AI

CivicPulse AI bridges the civic transparency gap by giving everyday citizens, journalists, and community leaders an instant, ground-truth verification engine:

* **Plain-Language Infrastructure Summaries:** Translates dense legal and economic terms (e.g., *capital expenditure*, *matching grants*) into concise, everyday bullet points.
* **Verifiable Direct Citations:** Every answer links directly to exact page numbers and verbatim text quotes from official government PDFs, preventing AI hallucinations and building trust.
* **Low-Bandwidth & Audio Accessibility:** Integrated text-to-speech engine (`gTTS`) generates instant audio breakdowns in local dialects/languages for screen-free and low-literacy access.
* **Pre-Loaded Civic Queries:** One-click shortcuts allow users to immediately extract data on solar streetlights, mini-grids, transformer distribution, and clean energy grants.
